Rebuild the D'Tunes Status video

Source: tools/status-video in the D-Tunes repo. Output: 30.09s, 1080x1920, 24fps (v9).

1. What you need

Google Chrome, Python 3 (standard library only) and ffmpeg. The soundtrack MP3 is not in the repo. Use the same file: audio-3ae9e05b.mp3 (54.12s).

2. Build

cd tools/status-video
./build.sh /path/to/audio-3ae9e05b.mp3 dtunes_status.mp4
# Windows: python build.py C:\path\to\audio-3ae9e05b.mp3 dtunes_status.mp4

It takes about 6 minutes. Chrome draws each of the 722 frames from scene.js; the audio is the first 30.09s with a fade out from 23.09 to 30.09; ffmpeg joins them.

3. Check it

The build is deterministic for one Chrome and ffmpeg version. Other versions can differ by a few pixels. Compare by eye with the delivered video, especially the D'Verse ending (from 23.3s).

4. Post it as a WhatsApp Status

  1. Move the MP4 to your phone.
  2. WhatsApp, Updates tab, add a status, pick the video from the gallery.
  3. Send it to your contacts. The video has no sound effects, only the soundtrack.

Change something

  • Copy: the SC list in scene.js (headline lines and subtitle per scene); intro and card text are in intro, card and boot.
  • Timing and motion: scene starts in SCN; each object's spot, size and arc in OBJS.
  • Look at one moment: python3 build.py --at 25.9, or serve the folder and open scene.html?t=25.9 in Chrome.
  • Ending: endPass in scene.js, a port of the D'Verse page's light engine and text effect.
Vibe Mix and offline are marked "Windows & web" in their subtitles because they are not confirmed on Android. Change that only after the Android build is checked.

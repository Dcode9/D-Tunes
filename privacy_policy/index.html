Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 27, 2026

Application ID: com.dcode9.dtunes

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how D'Tunes ("we", "us", or "our"), operated under the Dcode9 / D'Verse platform, collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application (Android & Wear OS package com.dcode9.dtunes) and web services available at tunes.d-verse.in.

We respect your privacy and are committed to maintaining a secure, transparent environment for all users.

2. Data We Collect & How We Use It

To deliver seamless music playback and cross-device synchronization, D'Tunes accesses and processes the following information:

  • Account Information: When you sign in using Google or D'Verse Auth, we securely store your email address and profile ID via Supabase Authentication to enable account sign-in and cloud sync across your devices.
  • Cloud Library & Playback History: If logged in, your liked songs, custom playlists, listening history, and playback statistics are stored in D'Verse Cloud (Supabase) to keep your feed and playlists synchronized across Android, Wear OS, and Web.
  • Local Device Audio Files: The Android app scans local audio files stored on your device to organize your offline music library. Your local music files remain 100% private on your device and are never uploaded or transmitted to any server.
  • Spotify Link Processing: When you paste a public Spotify URL to import playlists or songs, D'Tunes transiently reads public metadata (track titles, artist names, and cover art URLs) to locate matching tracks. We do not access or store your private Spotify credentials.

3. Android Device Permissions

D'Tunes requests the following permissions strictly to provide core app functionalities:

  • READ_MEDIA_AUDIO / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE: Used solely to detect and play local music files on your device.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Used to display media playback controls and current track information in your status bar.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE: Keeps audio playing smoothly in the background while you navigate other apps or lock your screen.

4. Third-Party Services & Infrastructure

D'Tunes integrates trusted third-party services to handle authentication, streaming, and cloud database operations:

  • Supabase (D'Verse Cloud): Handles encrypted authentication tokens and cloud database sync for playlists and history.
  • Google Identity Services: Provides optional secure Google Sign-In.
  • Public Music APIs: Used for fetching track metadata, album artwork, and streaming audio links.

We do NOT sell, rent, or monetize your personal information or listening habits to third-party advertisers or data brokers.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

You have full control over your data:

  • You can sign out at any time to remove cloud-synced data from your local device.
  • You can clear your search history and playback stats directly within the app settings.
  • To request permanent deletion of your D'Verse account and associated cloud data, please contact us at support@d-verse.in or submit a request directly via tunes.d-verse.in.

6. Children's Privacy

D'Tunes does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If we discover that personal information has been collected from a child under 13, we will promptly delete it.
